Privacy Policy

Your ideas stay yours.

Effective July 30, 2026

Relay is designed to help people ask better questions, examine AI answers, and keep useful ideas. This policy explains how Relay handles information, including Ask Relay and optional private cloud sync in Relay 1.2.

Local use

You can use Relay without turning on cloud sync. Notes and saved content are stored on your device first. Relay does not send existing private notes to its cloud database unless you choose to turn on Private cloud sync. Text you actively submit to Ask Relay is processed as described below.

Ask Relay and AI processing

When you submit a question to Ask Relay, Relay sends the question, your selected model and thinking lens, and the generated answer through Relay's server to provide the requested first answer, Agent checks, and synthesis. The selected AI provider—Google Gemini or OpenAI—processes this content to generate the result. Relay's API credentials remain on the server and are not stored in the app.

Please do not submit information that you do not want processed by these services. The first complete Relay result is available without an account. If you sign in, your Relay account identifier may be used to provide continued beta access and prevent abuse.

Optional private cloud sync

If you turn on Private cloud sync, Relay asks you to continue with Apple and creates a private Relay cloud account. Relay receives a stable account identifier from Apple so it can restore your account across your devices. Relay does not request your name, email address, or Apple password.

Relay sends the private content you choose to sync—such as questions, answers, topics, and follow-up questions—to your private cloud account. This content is not made public or shared with other Relay users by the current sync feature.

Service and security information

When you use Ask Relay or Private cloud sync, Relay and its infrastructure providers may process your IP address, device or app request information, usage limits, and authentication events such as sign-in, sign-out, and token refresh. We use this information only to operate the service, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and protect account security.

Service providers and location

Relay uses Supabase for authentication, database, and synchronization services; Cloudflare-based infrastructure for Relay's server; and Google Gemini or OpenAI for the AI response selected by the user. Relay's primary Supabase project is deployed in the Canada Central region. These providers and their infrastructure providers may process content and limited operational information where they operate in order to provide their services.

Retention and deletion

Your private cloud content is kept while your Relay cloud account remains active. You can delete the account and its private cloud content in Relay by going to Library → Private cloud sync → Manage → Erase cloud copy. This keeps the notes already stored on your device. Limited security logs and backup copies may remain temporarily until they expire through the cloud provider's ordinary retention cycle.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Relay does not use third-party advertising SDKs or track you across apps or websites. We do not sell personal information. The operational information described above is used only for app functionality, reliability, abuse prevention, and security.

Information you send us

If you contact support by email, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message. We use it only to respond, troubleshoot, and improve Relay.

Children's privacy

Relay is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

Relay will evolve. If our data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date before those changes apply.

Contact
